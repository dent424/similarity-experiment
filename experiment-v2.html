Instructions

Your Task

You will see pairs of brands, each shown as a short text description. Every brand in this study makes the same kind of drink. Because that is true of every pair you will see, it is not by itself a reason to call two brands similar. For each pair, rate how similar the two brands are in all the other ways they might be alike or different.

How to Rate Similarity

  • 0 = Not at all similar (nothing in common)
  • 100 = Extremely similar (nearly identical)
  • Values in between represent varying degrees of similarity

Important

  • There are no right or wrong answers - we want your honest opinion
  • You cannot go back to previous pairs
